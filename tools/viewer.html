Words used here

OFDM: the signal is many narrow subcarriers sent side by side, like Wi-Fi. Symbol: one time slot of all subcarriers together (28.8 µs). Pilots: points whose value the receiver knows, for measuring the channel. Constellation: the grid of allowed points; each carries 2, 4 or 6 bits. Error correction (LDPC): extra bits that let the receiver repair wrong bits.
